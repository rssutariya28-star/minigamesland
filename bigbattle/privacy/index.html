Privacy Policy

For the iOS app Big Battle

The short version: there is no account and no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. To match you with a rival and run a live battle, the game sends your nickname, avatar and region to its game server, and your scores to your rival while you play. Nothing identifies you personally, and matchmaking records are deleted within a day.

No account needed

You never give us an email address, phone number, password or real name. When you first look for a battle, the app quietly creates an anonymous guest account on the game server: a random ID, with a sign-in token kept on your device. It is not linked to your Apple ID or to any other account.

What stays on your device

Your nickname, your chosen animal avatar and the guest sign-in token are saved on your device so they are there next time. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What the game server receives, and why

Big Battle uses Supabase as its game server, hosted in Mumbai, India. It receives only what matchmaking and live battles need:

WhatWhyHow long it is kept
Your guest ID, nickname, avatar, a two-letter region code taken from your device's language and region settings, and your rating To find you a rival close to your rating, and to show your rival who they are playing (nickname, avatar, flag and rating) While you search. Your place in the queue is removed as soon as you are matched or leave; a place your app abandons is cleared within 10 minutes. Match records are deleted after one day.
Your score in each round, when you reached it, and, in some games, where you are (such as your car on the race track or which golf hole you are on) To show your rival your progress live, and so both phones agree on who won each round Not stored. It passes through the server straight to your rival's phone during the battle.
Your guest ID So you keep the same guest account next time Until you ask us to delete it. It holds no personal details.
Standard technical logs, such as IP address and the time of each request Kept by Supabase to run the service securely For a limited time, as described in Supabase's privacy policy

Your rival sees your nickname, avatar, region flag and rating, and your live scores during the battle. Please don't use your real name as your nickname.

What Big Battle does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No in-app purchases. The app never asks for payment details.
  • No location services, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Your choices

  • Change your nickname or avatar at any time on the home screen.
  • Delete everything on your device by uninstalling the app.
  • Ask for your guest account to be deleted by opening an issue on the issue tracker. Matchmaking records are deleted automatically within a day either way.

Children

Big Battle is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data? Open an issue on the issue tracker and mention Big Battle. It will be answered there.

Last updated: 20 October 2026. Applies to Big Battle 1.0 and later.
