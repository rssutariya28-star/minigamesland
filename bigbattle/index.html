Big Battle

Live 1v1 mini-game battles. Three rounds, three games, win two.

Coming soon to the App Store iPhone · free

No account, no sign-up. Pick a hero, enter a nickname, and play.

How a battle works

Every battle is best of three, and every round is a different game.

1

Find a rival

Tap Find a battle and you're matched with a rival near your rating, usually in seconds.

2

Three games, drawn at random

Each round explains its rules, waits until both players are ready, then counts down 3-2-1.

3

First to two wins

Scores go up live on both sides. Win two rounds and the battle is yours. Every battle earns XP toward your next level, and every win counts on the daily and weekly leaderboard.

The games

Twelve games, three per battle, in a new mix every time.

🏁

Lap race

One lap, top-down. Hold left or right to steer; first across the line wins.

🧸

Match 3D

30 seconds. Tap toys from the heap into your tray; three of a kind clear.

🔪

Knife throw

30 seconds. Tap to throw into a spinning wheel. Hit another knife and lose 2 points.

🏹

Archery

Three arrows, 10 seconds each, at a target sliding on a rail. Mind the wind.

✏️

Quick sketch

30 seconds. Draw the shape on show; closer and faster scores more.

⛳

Mini golf

Three holes. Putt around walls and sliding blocks in as few strokes as you can.

🔢

Number rush

30 seconds. Tap 1, 2, 3 and up, in order, on a shuffled grid.

🚗

Road hop

30 seconds. Hop across roads and rivers; every new row scores.

🌉

Bridge dash

30 seconds. Hold to grow a bridge, let go to drop it across the gap.

🏀

Basketball

30 seconds. Flick the ball at the hoop: 2 points, or 3 for a swish.

🥤

Cup pong

30 seconds. Flick a ball down the table into the cups. Clear the rack for a bonus.

🎯

Darts

Six darts at a real board, 5 seconds each. Hold steady, then let go.

Fair by design

Same targets for both

Both players get the same games in the same order, the same shapes, grids, traffic, wheels, winds and holes. Only skill decides.

A tie is a tie

Level on points? Nobody takes the round. Both phones always agree on the result.
