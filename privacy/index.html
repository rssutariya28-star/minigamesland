Privacy Policy

This page explains what Mini Games Land does with your information. The short version: your game progress stays on your phone, and the only data that may be shared is what is needed to show ads and keep the app running.

Your game data stays on your device

Your scores, coins, daily missions, login streak, and unlocked items are saved only on your own device. This data is not sent to us and is not shared with anyone. If you uninstall the app, it is removed from your device.

Ads

To keep the app free, Mini Games Land may show ads through Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob and its partners may receive limited information from your device, such as:

  • An advertising identifier (used to show ads and limit repeats)
  • General device and app information, like device type and app version
  • A rough, non-precise location based on that information

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time in your phone settings. On Apple devices you will also be asked whether to allow tracking, and you can say no. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Basic analytics

The app may record simple, anonymous events, such as which games are opened or how often the app is launched. This helps us understand what people enjoy and fix problems. These events are not used to identify you personally.

No account needed

There are no accounts and no logins. You do not give us a name, email, or password to play.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any ads shown are intended to follow the rules that apply to family-friendly apps.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.
