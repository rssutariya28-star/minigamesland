Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mini Games Land handles your information. In short: it does not collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. Mini Games Land does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline.

Your game data

Your scores, coins, daily missions, login streak, and unlocked items are saved only on your own device. This data never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your device.

Accounts and tracking

There are no accounts and no logins. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds anything that handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email the issue tracker.
