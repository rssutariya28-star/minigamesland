Privacy Policy

For the iOS app Mini Games: Offline Fun (Mini Games Land)

This page explains what Mini Games Land does with your information. The short version: there is no account, your game progress stays on your phone, and the only data that leaves your device is what Google needs in order to show ads, count anonymous usage, and report a crash.

Your game data stays on your device

Your scores, coins, daily missions, login streak, and unlocked items are saved only on your own device. This data is not sent to us and is not shared with anyone. If you uninstall the app, it is removed from your device.

No account needed

There are no accounts and no logins. You never give us a name, email address, phone number, password, or any other detail that identifies you personally.

What is collected, and by whom

The app itself does not run a server. Three Google services are built into it, and each collects a limited amount of information:

ServiceWhat it is forWhat it may receive
Google AdMob Shows the ads that pay for the app, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for coins. An advertising identifier, general device and app details (model, OS version, language, region, app version), and a rough, non-precise location derived from them.
Firebase Analytics Counts anonymous, aggregated usage — which games are opened, how long a session lasts, whether a mission was completed. An app instance identifier and event counts. It is not used to identify you personally.
Firebase Crashlytics Reports crashes so they can be found and fixed. The crash itself plus the device state around it: device model, OS version, app version, and how long the app had been running.

Google processes this in line with the Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services, and Firebase's privacy documentation. We only ever see it in aggregate, and we do not sell it.

Your choices

  • Tracking. On Apple devices the app asks, through Apple's own prompt, whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can say no, and you can change your answer later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent in Europe. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form appears before any ad is shown, and your choices there are respected.
  • Advertising identifier. You can reset it or limit ad personalisation at any time, under Settings → Privacy & Security on your device.
  • Delete everything. Uninstalling the app removes all of the progress stored on your device.

Ratings

After a good run the app may ask whether you would like to rate it. That prompt is shown by Apple, not by us — we are never told what you tapped or what you rated.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any ads shown are configured to follow the rules that apply to family-friendly apps.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data? Open an issue on the Mini Games Land issue tracker and it will be answered there.

Last updated: September 2026. Applies to app version 1.2.1 and later.
