Privacy Policy

For the iOS app No WiFi Mini Games Box (Mini Games Land)

This page explains what Mini Games Land does with your information. The short version: there is no account, your game progress stays on your phone, and the only data that leaves your device is what Google needs in order to show ads, count anonymous usage, report a crash, and tell the app whether a newer version is out.

Your game data stays on your device

Everything you build up while playing is saved only on your own device: your scores and bests, coins and diamonds, ranks and titles, themes and power-ups, daily and weekly challenges, your login streak, your settings, and the player name you choose, if you choose one. This data is not sent to us and is not shared with anyone. Updating the app keeps it. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device.

No account needed

There are no accounts and no logins. You never give us an email address, phone number, password, or any other detail that identifies you personally. A player name, if you set one, never leaves your phone.

No purchases

The app has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment details. Coins and diamonds are earned by playing, or by choosing to watch a video.

What is collected, and by whom

The app itself does not run a server. Four Google services are built into it, and each collects a limited amount of information:

ServiceWhat it is forWhat it may receive
Google AdMob Shows the ads that pay for the app: banners, a full-screen ad now and then when you play again, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for diamonds, a power-up, a theme trial, or to enter the Challenge of the Day. An advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), general device and app details (model, OS version, language, region, app version), and a rough, non-precise location derived from them.
Firebase Analytics Counts anonymous, aggregated usage: which games are played and for how long, which features are used (challenges, themes, power-ups, the lucky wheel), how ads performed, and general settings such as whether sound is on or tracking was allowed. An app instance identifier and event counts. It is not used to identify you personally.
Firebase Crashlytics Reports crashes so they can be found and fixed. The crash itself plus the device state around it: device model, OS version, app version, and how long the app had been running.
Firebase Remote Config Tells the app whether a newer version is available or required, and can adjust a few game settings (such as rank targets) without an update. A Firebase installation identifier and basic app and device details, such as app version, platform and language.

Google processes this in line with the Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services, and Firebase's privacy documentation. We only ever see it in aggregate, and we do not sell it.

Your choices

  • Tracking. On Apple devices the app asks, through Apple's own prompt, whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can say no, and you still get every game either way. You can change your answer later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent in Europe. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form appears before any ad is shown, and your choices there are respected. You can change them at any time from Privacy choices in the app's Settings.
  • Rewarded videos are optional. Every game can be played without ever watching one.
  • Advertising identifier. You can reset it or limit ad personalisation at any time, under Settings → Privacy & Security on your device.
  • Delete everything. Uninstalling the app removes all of the progress stored on your device.

Ratings

After a good run the app may ask whether you would like to rate it. That prompt is shown by Apple, not by us, and we are never told what you tapped or what you rated.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data? Open an issue on the Mini Games Land issue tracker and it will be answered there.
