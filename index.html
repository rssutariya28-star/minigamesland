Twenty games, one tap away

Arcade, puzzle and reflex games, all in the same app. Pick something and play for two minutes or twenty. Every game keeps your best score, so there is always something to beat.

Stack Rush

Tap to drop each block. Anything hanging over the edge is sliced off.

Hex Merge

Drop tiles onto the grid. Two equal numbers join into a bigger one.

Bounce Blitz

Place pads under the ball and never let it reach the floor.

Slice Storm

Swipe through the fruit for combos. Never touch a bomb.

Neon Snake

The classic, lit up. Open edges at first, then walls and shapes.

Drift Racer

Steer with your thumb, miss the barriers, take the coins.

Sky Jump

Bounce upward for ever. Cracked ledges only hold you once.

Lane Hop

Cross the roads one row at a time. You can always stop and wait.

Pulse Sprint

Endless runner. Jump, slide, and keep going — one hit ends it.

Brick Smash

Serve the ball, move the paddle, break every brick.

Orbit Dodge

Circle the middle without stopping. Tap to flip the other way.

Color Sort

Pour colours between tubes until every tube holds just one.

Tap Dash

Tap to jump, tap again in the air, clear the gaps and spikes.

Block Drop

Slide and turn the falling pieces. Fill a whole row to clear it.

Memory Match

Turn two cards at a time and find every matching pair.

Bridge Dash

Hold to grow the plank. Let go at exactly the right length.

Spin Strike

Fire pins into the spinning wheel without hitting one already there.

Bubble Pop

Aim, shoot, and group three or more of a colour to clear them.

Pipe Twist

Turn the pipes a quarter at a time to join the source to every drain.

Hoop Toss

Turn the phone sideways and drag to shoot. Three misses ends the run.

Good to know

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every one of the twenty games plays fully offline. A connection is only used, when you happen to have one, to show ads and to send anonymous usage and crash reports.

Do I need to make an account?

No. There is no login or sign up of any kind, and you never give us a name or an email to play.

Where is my progress saved?

On your own device. Scores, coins, missions, streaks and unlocked items are stored on the phone and are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Is it free? Are there ads?

The app is free. It is paid for by ads, and there are optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for extra coins. Nothing is locked behind a purchase.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. It runs on iPhone and iPad, iOS 15 and later.

Something is broken, or I want a game added

Open an issue on the Mini Games Land issue tracker. Bug reports and suggestions are both welcome.
