The games

Twenty-four games, one tap away

Arcade, puzzle and reflex games in the same app. Play one for two minutes or twenty. Every game keeps your best score, has its own music, and shows you how it plays the first few times.

Stack Rush

Stack blocks. Chain perfects.

Tap to drop each block. Line it up exactly for a perfect, and keep the perfects coming.

Hex Merge

Merge tiles. Build chains.

Drag tiles onto the hex board. Equal numbers merge into a bigger one, and chains pay big.

Bounce Blitz

Bounce on monsters. Stomp them all.

Steer your bouncing ball onto the monsters. Tap to slam down, and chain stomp after stomp.

Slice Storm

Swipe to slice. Avoid the bombs.

Slash through the fruit for combos. Cut right beside a bomb for a risk bonus, but never the bomb.

Neon Snake

Eat orbs. Slip through the gaps.

The classic, lit up. Edges wrap at first, then walls close in with gaps to slip through.

Drift Racer

Hold to steer. Chain your drifts.

Weave past the barriers and grab the coins. Hold through a lane change to drift and build a combo.

Sky Jump

Drag to steer. Climb the platforms.

Your hero bounces on its own. Guide it up, hit the springs, and watch for ledges that give way.

Lane Hop

Hop forward. Dodge the cars.

Tap to hop, swipe to side-step. No rush: wait on the grass until the road has a gap.

Pulse Sprint

Jump. Slide. Sprint forever.

An endless runner, played sideways. Jump the blocks, slide under the gates, grab every gold orb.

Brick Smash

Blast the bouncing bricks.

Hold to fire and slide to move. Big bricks split in two, so don't let one land on you.

Orbit Dodge

Hold left or right. Slip through the rings.

Move your ship round the middle and be under the gap when each ring closes in.

Color Sort

Pour colors. Sort the tubes.

Tap a tube, then another, to pour. Finish when every tube holds one colour. Undo is free.

Meteor GuardNEW

Tap the sky. Save the towns.

Fire at the falling meteors. Shots take a moment to climb, so aim ahead and keep your towns standing.

Block Drop

Drop blocks. Clear the rows.

Drag to move, tap to turn, flick down to slam. Fill a row to clear it, and clear a gem to set it off.

Memory Match

Flip cards. Find every pair.

Turn two cards at a time. Pairs in a row add time to the clock, and a wrong guess costs some.

Bridge Dash

Hold to build. Cross the gap.

Hold to grow the plank and let go to drop it across. Land on the gold centre for a perfect.

Spin Strike

Throw pins. Never clash.

Tap to throw a pin into the spinning wheel. Never hit a pin that is already there.

Wall FlipNEW

Tap to leap. Dodge the spikes.

Leap from wall to wall, and tap again in the air to turn back. Keep off the spikes and chain the stars.

Pipe Twist

Rotate pipes. Complete the flow.

Turn the pipes to join the source to every drain before the time runs out.

Hoop Toss

Drag to aim. Sink the shot.

Played sideways. Drag and let go to shoot. Nothing but net scores double.

Zone GrabNEW

Draw a line. Claim the land.

Swipe out, draw a line, and get back to claim what you cut off. Don't let an enemy touch your line.

Fish FrenzyNEW

Eat small fish. Grow big.

Drag to swim. Eat the smaller fish, swerve away from the bigger ones, and keep growing.

Hill GlideNEW

Hold to dive. Beat the sunset.

Hold to dive down each hill and let go to fly off the top. Land on a downslope to keep your speed.

Tap Dash

Tap to jump. Run forever.

Played sideways. Tap to jump, tap again to double-jump, and swipe down to drop out of a jump.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All 24 games play fully offline. When you happen to have a connection, it is used to show ads (including the optional videos for diamonds, power-ups and theme trials), to send anonymous usage and crash reports, and to check whether a newer version of the app is out.

Do I need to make an account?

No. There is no login or sign up of any kind. You can give yourself a player name if you like, and it stays on your phone.

Where is my progress saved?

On your own device. Scores, coins, diamonds, ranks, themes, power-ups, challenges and your streak are stored on the phone and are not sent anywhere. Updating the app keeps them; deleting the app removes them.

Is it free? Are there ads?

The app is free, and there are no in-app purchases. It is paid for by ads. Coins and diamonds are earned by playing, and you can choose to watch a short video for extras such as diamonds, a power-up or a theme trial.

Why is it called something else on the App Store?

On the App Store it is listed as No WiFi Mini Games Box. It is the same app: once installed, it is called Mini Games Land.

Does it work on iPad?

It is made for iPhone and needs iOS 15 or later. iPads can install it from the App Store too, and run it in the iPhone layout.

Something is broken, or I want a game added

Open an issue on the Mini Games Land issue tracker. Bug reports and suggestions are both welcome.
